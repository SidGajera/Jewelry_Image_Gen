# LR-0198 "Anastasia" — Source Spec (locked, verified from CADs 1–4.png)

**Product:** 2ct Elongated Hexagon SIX-CLAW Solitaire, Hidden Halo, Cathedral High Setting.
**Metal:** 18k Yellow Gold, single tone. **Stone:** elongated hexagon step-cut ~13×6.5mm, 2.00ct (very slim, L:W ~2:1). ~16 hidden-halo side stones, band 1.8mm.

## Geometry locks
1. **Stone = ELONGATED HEXAGON step-cut, POINTED top AND bottom (chevron ends), very slim, set EAST-WEST** (long axis across the finger). Two long parallel sides tapering to a point at each end. NOT emerald, NOT octagon, NOT cushion, NO flat horizontal top/bottom edge. Source CAD is the exact truth.
2. **Setting = SIX single CLAWS (prongs)** holding the stone at its hexagon corners — smooth rounded/tapered single claws (NOT double/split, NOT eagle-tip). **PRONG-SET, NOT a bezel** (this is the key difference vs LR-0197 which is bezel). Exactly 6 claws.
3. **HIDDEN HALO:** a single row of small round pavé tucked UNDER the head/basket, following the outline — visible only from side / three-quarter / back. NEVER a halo on the top face.
4. **CATHEDRAL HIGH setting:** the shank rises to the head; stone elevated; open gallery/basket underneath.
5. **Band:** thin plain rounded yellow-gold shank ~1.8mm, gently tapering. No pavé, no milgrain, no twist. Head centered & square on band.
