# LR-0195 (Hexagon) — Source Spec

**Product:** Three-stone engagement ring, 18k YELLOW GOLD. Elongated hexagon center + 2 tapered-baguette sides.

## Geometry locks (verified against CAD front/side 2026-07-27)
1. **Center = ELONGATED HEXAGON step-cut diamond.** SIX sides; POINTED chevron point at BOTH top AND bottom; long axis vertical; step-cut facets. NOT a flat-top emerald. Flat top/bottom = emerald = REJECT.
2. **Two SIDE stones = TAPERED BAGUETTE diamonds** — long, straight, tapering; one each side flanking the center, tapering down toward the shoulders (not kites, not rounds).
3. **Prongs:** center hexagon held by DOUBLE / split-claw prongs at its corners; tapered baguettes bar/prong-set along the shoulders. Match count/style exactly.
4. **Band:** thin, high-polish 18k yellow gold, knife-edge/cathedral shoulders. Single warm gold tone.

## HEXAGON RULES (mandatory — emerald-drift is the #1 failure)
- Center MUST show TWO sharp points (top + bottom) and six sides in every render.
- NEVER shoot a pure flat side profile — use a 3/4 angle (~30–45° off profile) so the pointed top+bottom stay visible.
- Validate "pointed top + pointed bottom visible, not flat" BEFORE delivering; reject + regen on any emerald read.
