## Geometry locks (verified against source renders + wrist shot 2026-07-28)
1. **Repeating floral unit** — the row is a continuous single line of **4-marquise "flower" clusters** (four marquise-cut diamonds arranged as an X / four-petal flower) **alternating with a single ROUND BRILLIANT** diamond that sits between the flowers on the centerline. 2. **Cut mix (exact):** marquise (navette) petals + round brilliant connectors ONLY. No princess/emerald/pear/oval. Do not simplify the flowers to plain rounds or a single straight line.
3. **Setting:** marquise petals **prong-set at the tips** (bright white metal claws); rounds in small **prong baskets**. Uniform repeating claw pattern. Do not restyle to bezel/channel.
4. **Metal:** WHITE — rhodium-bright / high-polish, single white tone throughout. NOT yellow/rose gold.
5. **Underside / gallery:** open **X / infinity openwork link gallery** connecting the flower units; flexible articulated line that drapes on the wrist.
6. **Clasp:** rectangular **box clasp** with a **figure-8 / safety-eight latch** (visible laid open/flat).
7. Width ≈ one flower head; even floral rhythm, no gaps, no missing/extra petals.
