# LR-0193 (Hexagon) — Source Spec

**Product:** Three-stone engagement ring, 18k YELLOW GOLD. Elongated hexagon center + 2 kite side stones.

## Geometry locks (verified against CAD front 2026-07-27)
1. **Center = ELONGATED HEXAGON step-cut diamond.** SIX sides; POINTED chevron point at BOTH top AND bottom; long axis vertical; step-cut (emerald-style) facets. NOT a flat-top emerald. Flat top/bottom = emerald = REJECT.
2. **FOUR KITE side stones (TWO per side), NOT two.** Each side of the hexagon has a SMALLER kite at the UPPER shoulder + a LARGER kite at the LOWER shoulder, all pointed/step-cut, points oriented outward — forming a symmetric 5-stone cluster (1 hexagon + 4 kites). (Corrected 2026-07-27 from real on-hand photo; CAD undercounted.)
3. **Prongs:** center hexagon held by DOUBLE / split-claw prongs at its corners (paired claws); side kites prong-set. Match count/style exactly; no added/single-claw restyle.
4. **Band:** thin, high-polish 18k yellow gold, CATHEDRAL shoulders rising to the center with an open under-gallery. Single warm gold tone.

## HEXAGON RULES (mandatory — emerald-drift is the #1 failure)
- Center MUST show TWO sharp points (top + bottom) and six sides in every render.
- NEVER shoot a pure flat side profile — use a 3/4 angle (~30–45° off profile) so the pointed top+bottom stay visible.
- Validate "pointed top + pointed bottom visible, not flat" BEFORE delivering; reject + regen on any emerald read.
