### REQUIRED PRODUCTION METHOD — D2D CAD-COMPOSITE (preferred when the CAD file + a renderer are available)
**The jewelry must NEVER be generated by the image model.** The source CAD is the only jewelry authority. AI is used ONLY for background, cloth folds, lighting, shadows, reflections, human model, and scene composition — never to generate, redraw, rotate, reconstruct, repair, extend, inpaint or reinterpret the jewelry.

Method: (1) import the original CAD/STL/3DM into a CAD renderer (Blender / Rhino / KeyShot / MatrixGold); (2) render the exact required camera angle from the CAD, preserving head, centre basket, horizontal support bar, V-gallery, side pear baskets, side-stone tilt/spacing, cathedral supports, triangular shoulder openings, prongs, negative spaces, band taper/thickness, full silhouette; (3) export the jewelry render with transparency/clean mask; (4) generate the environment separately; (5) composite the UNTOUCHED CAD render into the scene; (6) add physically correct contact shadows/reflections without warping the jewelry; (7) apply the preserved logo separately (owner `04`); (8) validate the composite against the matching CAD angle.

**PROHIBITED for jewelry geometry:** sending the source ring to an image model for another angle · image-to-image rotation · generative fill/inpaint on the ring · AI reconstruction of hidden geometry · AI side/rear synthesis · AI-redrawn prongs/baskets/gallery/stones/shoulders/band · prompt-only "CAD lock" workflows.

**SIDE / REAR / THREE-QUARTER RULE:** produce such an angle ONLY when that exact angle is rendered from the CAD, or a sufficiently close CAD render can be composited without inventing hidden structure. **If the CAD cannot provide the angle, do NOT generate that jewelry angle with AI — skip it.**

**VALIDATION GATE:** overlay the final jewelry on the CAD render at the same angle; require exact alignment of outer + inner silhouette, centre-head height/width, gallery openings, horizontal bar, central V, side-stone positions, side baskets, prongs, cathedral supports, shoulder openings, band profile, metal thickness, negative spaces. Any mismatch = automatic rejection.

## 6. FAILURE → AUTO-FALLBACK (never ship a redesign)
Pure generation cannot guarantee 99.9% CAD fidelity — `nano_banana` biases toward the reference but re-synthesizes geometry. On ANY ✗, do not deliver and switch automatically (never ask which method), per `config/QUALITY_MEMORY.json geometry-immutable-auto-fallback`:
1. **FALLBACK 1** — composite the real source-ring pixels into the AI scene: `scripts/composite_ring_into_scene.py` (matches lighting/shadows/reflections/perspective/DoF). Pixel-exact geometry, 0 credits.
2. **FALLBACK 2** — if the requested camera angle is unreachable by compositing, render the exact geometry at that angle from the CAD file (when available), then composite onto cloth.

The composite runs where the render is downloadable (the user's machine / the `tool/` backend) — that is the point of the tool: it unblocks downloads so this fallback is automatic rather than manual.
