## SOURCE JEWELRY LOCK
The 4-view source CAD is the ONLY authority. Never add, remove, redesign, reconstruct, beautify, simplify, correct, or invent any jewelry detail. Preserve exactly:
- Center diamond shape, size, cut, crown, table, pavilion, orientation and position
- Side-diamond and pavé count, size, spacing, coverage and placement
- Prong count, shape, thickness, angle, length and position
- Head, basket, gallery, bridge, cathedral, setting and under-gallery
- Band width, thickness, curves, bends, taper, narrowing, widening and silhouette
- Metal thickness, proportions, structure and finish

Never add extra prongs, diamonds, gold, supports, head details, halos or gallery elements. Never remove existing features. If a bend, taper or widening exists, preserve it exactly; if it does not exist, never invent it. **Only the CAMERA ANGLE may change.**

## PREMIUM CLOTH LOCK
Only the approved premium pure neutral-white cotton cloth: fine realistic cotton weave · soft natural sheen · elegant natural folds · soft neutral daylight · natural contact shadows · luxury jewelry-photography quality. Never cheap, plain, synthetic, rough, grey, cream, beige, ivory, yellow, pink, blue or color-tinted cloth.

## OFFICIAL LOGO LOCK
Exactly ONE official preserved Lucent Carat Lab logo. Preserve exactly: diamond icon · typography · layout · kerning · alignment · gold colors and gradients · BLACK tagline · line thickness · spacing and scale ratio.
Never allow: missing logo · partial/half-cut logo · multiple or ghost logos · recreated/fake logo · gold tagline · floating/pasted/sticker/watermark/embossed appearance · white box, glow or separate logo shadow · logo that ignores cloth weave/folds/perspective · **oversized logo, enlarged emblem, or any background diamond outline/wireframe/line-art** (recurring failure — see QUALITY_MEMORY logo-method-inmodel-higgsfield-only + lr0180-inmodel-logo-overlay-and-duplicate).
The complete logo stays inside the frame unless natural jewelry occlusion is intentional. It must look physically printed into the cotton fibers and share the cloth's texture, folds, perspective, lighting and depth of field. Keep it SMALL and secondary (lower area, clear of the ring).

## JEWELRY-FIRST FOCUS
Jewelry is always the primary subject — the sharpest, brightest, most detailed element. The logo must never compete with it. When the logo is near/behind the jewelry, apply only natural camera depth-of-field blur to the complete preserved logo; blur must never alter its design, typography, colors, black tagline, layout or proportions.
