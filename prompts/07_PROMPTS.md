Do NOT redesign, reinterpret, approximate, beautify, or improve any part of the ring. Keep IDENTICAL to the source: overall silhouette + proportions; center halo geometry (exact halo shape — a round halo stays perfectly circular, never cushion/rounded-square); center-stone diameter; halo diameter + halo thickness + halo stone count; prong count + shape + positions; band width + thickness; infinity/twist side-loop geometry + the open negative-space between strands; shoulder curvature + shoulder→halo transition; pavé layout + spacing + stone size + start/end positions; stone count; stone size; stone orientation; gallery profile; metal thickness; left/right symmetry. ONLY change the background, lighting, shadows, reflections, environment, camera angle and depth of field — treat the jewelry itself as a locked, immutable object. If any part of the jewelry cannot be preserved exactly, do NOT invent or redesign it — preserve the original geometry even if that means less dramatic lighting. The result must look like the EXACT SAME physical ring moved into a photoshoot, never a reinterpreted version.
> **ZERO JEWELRY INVENTION.** Never ADD, remove, or modify any component that is not in the source: no added accent diamonds, hidden halo, pavé/diamond bridge, gallery/"surprise" diamonds, extra prongs, extra galleries, added metal supports, filigree, split shank, cathedral conversion, basket redesign, or stone-count change. If a feature is not visible in the source, DO NOT create it — missing stays missing; never "improve" or add luxury details (docs/16).
> **HEAD & GALLERY GEOMETRY LOCK.** The center-stone assembly is immutable: preserve exactly the prong count/thickness/angle/position, basket + gallery + bridge + under-gallery geometry, head height/width/thickness, metal volume, and every opening. Never add a support ring, girdle rail, gallery rail, metal collar, extra bridge, additional basket, thicker head, or ANY structural metal not in the source. Keep the head's exact silhouette and the diamond's exact seating (docs/16).
>
> Preserve the exact ring design, stone shape, stone count, setting, prongs, band proportions and metal details from the source image.
> Use realistic VVS-quality lab-grown diamonds, accurate refraction, natural faceting, premium polished metal, luxury jewellery photography, sharp focus, clean background and commercial product-image quality.
> Do not redesign, simplify, add stones, remove stones, alter proportions, change the setting or modify the band.

Per shot, prepend an ANGLE/scene tag and append the group wrapper + the DESIGN string for the specific SKU. Keep the no-doubled-diamond clause. Everything 1:1, 2K.

## SOURCE LOCKING — ROOT FIX (user-locked 2026-07-24)
**ALWAYS pass the source CAD image as a locked visual reference. NEVER build the ring from text imagination.** The CAD is the thing being preserved; the scene plate only donates background, lighting, camera and white balance. Put the CAD first in `medias` and say so in the prompt, so the model treats the ring as the base to keep rather than as one influence among several.

**Only the camera angle may change.** Everything else matches the source shape-for-shape.

**Never ADD a halo, pavé or accent stone that is not in the source. Never REMOVE one that is.** If a reading of the CAD would add a component, confirm it before writing it into a prompt — do not invent accents on your own reading. (Learned fail: `lr-0194-is-a-plain-solitaire-no-accents`.)

### DO NOT DESCRIBE THE SETTING IN WORDS (learned fail 2026-07-24)
**Never state prong count, prong style, gallery form or basket form in the prompt text.** Any such description is your reading of the CAD, not the CAD — and when prose and reference image disagree, the model follows the prose. A guess in the prompt beats the truth in the image.

For prongs, gallery, basket and band the prompt says only:

> Reproduce the prongs, gallery, basket and band EXACTLY as they appear in the reference CAD — same count, same shape, same style, same thickness, same positions. Do not add, remove, split, double, restyle, thicken, thin or reposition any of them. Copy what is in the image.

Reserve prose for what the image cannot carry — scene, background, lighting, camera, physics — and for exclusions the **user** has stated. (Learned fail: `never-describe-the-setting-in-words-copy-it-from-the-cad`.)

## STONE SHAPE LOCK — READ THE SOURCE FIRST (user-locked 2026-07-24, CRITICAL)
**Read the source CAD/image BEFORE writing the prompt.** Match EVERY stone's exact **shape, count, size and placement** — centre, sides and accents. Never substitute, guess or "beautify" a shape. A tapered baguette stays a tapered baguette; an elongated hexagon stays an elongated hexagon.

**Name each cut explicitly, describe its defining outline, and name the shapes it must NOT become.** Fancy shapes collapse toward commoner neighbours unless the prompt blocks it — an elongated hexagon will drift to emerald or radiant on its own.

> STONE SHAPE: the centre is an ELONGATED HEXAGON — SIX straight sides, two long parallel sides with angled clipped corners at each end, long axis running NORTH-SOUTH ALONG the finger. It is NOT an emerald cut, NOT radiant, NOT oval, NOT cushion, NOT octagonal. Reproduce the six-sided outline exactly.

**Orientation — determine it from a WORN photo or an AXIAL CAD view, never from side-stone placement.** An axial view is one where the band reads as a full circle with the hole visible; there the in-image horizontal is ACROSS the finger. For the hexagon family the centre is set **EAST-WEST — long axis ACROSS the finger, perpendicular to the band** (confirmed against the LR-0194 worn photos: band horizontal, stone standing tall).

> Do NOT infer centre orientation from where the side stones sit. These are east-west three-stone designs: the centre's long axis is perpendicular to the finger *while* the side stones sit on the shoulders along the finger. Both are true at once, so side stones tell you nothing about the centre.

**Pre-delivery gate:** verify every stone's shape against the source. Any mismatch = REJECT, record in Failure Memory, do not deliver. See `config/QUALITY_MEMORY.json#stone-shape-must-match-source-exactly`.

## DIAMOND CLARITY BLOCK — MANDATORY IN EVERY PROMPT (user-locked 2026-07-24)
> DIAMOND CLARITY: every diamond rendered with FULL clarity — facets, cut, shape, angles, table, crown and pavilion all crisp and clearly visible, exactly matching each stone's true shape from the source (round, baguette, etc.).
