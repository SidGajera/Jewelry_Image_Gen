Our Heritage

Born in Surat —
the diamond city of the world.

Lunivae was founded where the world's diamonds are cut and polished. For more than fourteen years we have grown from a family workshop into fully owned manufacturing units, quietly crafting for clients and houses across continents.

We keep everything under one roof — the design, the casting, the setting, the finish — because that is the only way to guarantee the standard our name is built on. Royal in inspiration, modern in intent, personal in every detail.
