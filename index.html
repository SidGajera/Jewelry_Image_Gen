Understanding Gold Karats (10K vs 14K vs 18K)

We work exclusively in **Solid Karat Gold**. Pure gold is 24K, but is too soft for jewelry, bending and scratching easily. By alloying gold with high-quality metals, we produce durable, stunning rings for everyday wear.

18K Solid Gold

75.0% Pure Gold. Offers a deep, rich yellow tone. The standard for premium European luxury. Slightly softer but highly prestigious.

14K Solid Gold

58.3% Pure Gold. The absolute sweet spot for engagement rings in the USA. Offers beautiful luster combined with high structural durability.

10K Solid Gold

41.7% Pure Gold. Incredibly robust and resistant to scratching. Perfect for active lifestyles and budget-conscious buyers seeking solid gold value.

Mined vs. Lab-Grown Diamonds

A common misconception in the US market is that lab-grown diamonds are "fake" like cubic zirconia or moissanite. In reality, **they are real diamonds** grown in controlled, high-tech chambers that replicate the extreme heat and pressure of the Earth's mantle.

Because they are created in controlled environments, lab-grown diamonds are often of higher average purity (Type IIa) than natural diamonds, which are prone to nitrogen inclusions.

Optically Identical: They reflect, refract, and disperse light exactly the same, carrying the same brilliance and sparkle (fire).
Physically Identical: They register a perfect 10 on the Mohs hardness scale. Only another diamond can scratch them.
IGI Certified: Independent gemologists grade both mined and lab diamonds on the exact same 4Cs criteria (Cut, Color, Clarity, Carat).
IGI CERTIFICATION STANDARDS

The Importance of Independent Verification

The International Gemological Institute (IGI) is one of the world's most trusted diamond grading authorities. Every diamond we offer is sent to an independent IGI laboratory, where a team of gemologists analyzes it using spectroscopic and microscopic analysis before generating an official certificate.

CARAT

The precise weight of the diamond. 1 carat equals 200 milligrams. Even minor differences in weight can alter pricing significantly.

COLOR

Grades the lack of color in a diamond. Ranging from D (completely colorless and rarest) to Z (noticeable yellow tint). We only use D-F (Colorless) or G-H (Near Colorless) diamonds.

CLARITY

Grades the microscopic natural characteristics inside the diamond (inclusions) and on its surface (blemishes). We source VVS1-VS2 stones, which are 100% eye-clean.

CUT

The most critical of the 4Cs. The cut determines how effectively the diamond reflects light. A perfect cut creates maximum fire. We use **Excellent** or **Ideal** cut grades exclusively.

Solid Gold vs. Gold Plated

Aura Fine Jewelry exclusively uses solid gold. Here is why it matters:

Aura Solid Gold

Gold is mixed with strong alloys all the way through. It never chips, flakes, or rubs off. It holds its value and can be polished for generations.

Plated / Vermeil Gold

A base metal (like brass or silver) dipped in a microscopic layer of gold. The gold wears off rapidly, exposing the dark metal underneath, turning skin green.

Lab-Grown vs. Earth-Mined Diamonds

The Federal Trade Commission (FTC) classification states that a diamond is pure crystalline carbon, regardless of whether it is mined from the ground or grown in a laboratory.

Lab Grown (CVD/HPHT)

  • Chemistry: 100% Crystalline Carbon
  • Refractive Index: 2.42 (Identical Brilliance)
  • Ethics: No land mining, zero carbon footprint
  • Cost: 30% - 50% lower price
  • Grading: Official IGI Certificate

Natural Mined

  • Chemistry: 100% Crystalline Carbon
  • Refractive Index: 2.42 (Identical Brilliance)
  • Ethics: Traditional mining practices
  • Cost: Premium rarity markup
  • Grading: Official IGI Certificate
